# Third-party assets

- `assets/hero/Monk.gltf` — Quaternius, RPG Character Pack (November 2020), CC0 1.0. Original free distribution: https://quaternius.com/packs/rpgcharacters.html . The glTF is self-contained and includes a skinned mesh, embedded texture, and animations. Downloaded from a public mirror of the unmodified source model: https://github.com/DarClsr/well_world/blob/main/assets/quaternius/characters/Monk.gltf . The game's script adds dark hair and selects locomotion/combat clips at runtime.

The active 2D protagonist and dojo art are original pixel art generated specifically for this project with `tools_make_pixel_art.py`; no third-party character model is used in the 2D build.
