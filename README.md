# Murim: Sendero del Qi

A small Android action RPG prototype built in Godot 4.4. The current build uses a top-down 2D pixel-art style with softly shaded character sprites and ground shadows for a semi-3D impression. Legacy 3D prototype files are excluded from the Android package.

## Controls
- Drag the left virtual stick to walk; push it toward the edge to run.
- Tap **Ataque** repeatedly to chain three forms: a quick cut, a rising cut, then an all-around Qi spin. Each leaves a layered, softly fading light trail; the combo resets if you pause for about one second.
- Tap **Especial** (or press E on desktop) for a full-body radiant Qi wave with warm mist, curved energy ribbons and seven soft petal flares. It keeps a radius three times the character's height, costs 30 Qi and has a five-second cooldown.
- Tap **Esquiva** to dodge along the stick direction, or backward if the stick is neutral.
- Tap **Salto** for a short hop.
- Drag the right side to pan the camera; pinch to zoom.
- On desktop, use WASD or arrow keys, Space to dodge, J to jump, and the left mouse button to attack.

Pixel character and dojo textures are generated by `tools_make_pixel_art.py` and use nearest-neighbor filtering; subtle sprite shading, soft contact shadows and layered glow strokes add a semi-3D feel while gameplay remains 2D.
